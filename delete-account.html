Kanzul Imaan — Privacy

Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. Use this page to delete your account or request removal of specific data.

Option 1 — In-App

Delete directly inside the app

The fastest way to delete your account is from within Kanzul Imaan:

  • 1
    Open Kanzul Imaan Launch the app on your Android device.
  • 2
    Go to Settings Tap the menu icon (☰) and select Settings.
  • 3
    Open Account Select Account → Delete Account.
  • 4
    Confirm deletion Read the confirmation and tap Delete my account. Your account and data will be removed within 30 days.
Option 2 — Online Request

Submit a deletion request

If you can't access the app, complete the form below. We'll process your request within 30 days and confirm by email.

✓ Request received. We'll send a confirmation to your email and complete the deletion within 30 days.
Data Retention Policy

What data is deleted or kept

The table below explains what happens to each type of data when you delete your account.

Data type Description On account deletion Retention after request
Account info Email address, username Deleted Within 30 days
Reading history Surahs and pages visited Deleted Within 30 days
Bookmarks & notes Saved verses, personal notes Deleted Within 30 days
App preferences Font, theme, translation settings Deleted Within 30 days
Crash & analytics logs Anonymised diagnostic data Retained Up to 90 days (anonymised)
Legal / billing records Purchase receipts (if applicable) Retained As required by law
